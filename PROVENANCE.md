# Project Provenance

## Origin

**Date:** 2026-09-18

The project began during an exploratory discussion about liberty, AI, and curiosity.

Robert Leo Duffy III proposed the formulation:

> **"curiosity as the sudden awareness of specific, attainable knowledge"**

That sentence is the originating observation for *Attainable Unknowns*.

It was not originally offered as a validated definition, scale, theory, or preregistered hypothesis.

## Development of the research idea

Subsequent discussion developed several empirical possibilities around the formulation:

- separating **specificity** from **attainability**;
- distinguishing reported curiosity from behavioral information-seeking;
- giving participants a limited number of opportunities to reveal answers;
- using a visual interface with large buttons, topic tiles, and sliders;
- comparing broad interest categories with much more specific categories;
- considering a hierarchical path from broad domain to specific topic to specific unknown;
- recruiting actual human participants through a platform such as Prolific;
- minimizing the usefulness of AI assistance by making participant choices, rather than generated prose, the principal data.

These are design developments around the originating observation. They should not be represented as having been present in the original sentence.

## Provenance rule

As the project develops, distinguish:

1. **originating observations and questions**;
2. **exploratory design decisions**;
3. **pilot findings**;
4. **preregistered confirmatory decisions**;
5. **post-data exploratory analyses**.

Later clarity should not be projected backward onto earlier uncertainty.
